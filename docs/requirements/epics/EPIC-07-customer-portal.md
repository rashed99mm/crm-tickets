## Goal

Allow customers to manage their support requests through a self-service portal *(rule
specification §8)*.

## Status: not specified

Slice S3 has no spec. Fixed constraints the future spec inherits: a customer sees only their own
tickets and only published articles (`BR-20`); satisfaction is one 1–5 question with optional text,
sent on resolution (`PA-3`); web forms enter here as a channel; accessibility is WCAG 2.1 AA
(`NFR-14`). Blocked on sprints 6 and 9 — replies need the message record, survey invitations need
the email provider.
